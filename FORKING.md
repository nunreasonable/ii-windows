# Forking

Illogical Windows is free software: the setup and the packaging scripts are GPL-3.0, ii for Windows is GPL-3.0 and Quickshell for Windows is LGPL-3.0. Anyone can fork, change and share them under those licenses, and nothing on this page takes those rights away. This page says which forks this project points people to and supports.

## Forks this project promotes

Forks that add customizations on top of Illogical Windows, the way [end4-pC](https://github.com/pctrade/end4-pC) adds its look and features on top of illogical-impulse on Linux:

- They're installed on top of Illogical Windows. [nunreasonable/ii-windows](https://github.com/nunreasonable/ii-windows) is installed first, with its own setup, and the fork goes on top of it instead of replacing it.
- They customize what's there (themes, presets, bar layouts, widgets, extra modules) instead of being an all-new shell or a separate install of their own.

Forks like that can be listed in the README and on the website, and talked about in this repo's issues and in the Discord. Forks that ship their own full install, or that are a new project rather than a customization, aren't listed or promoted here, and their problems can't be handled in this repo's issues.

## Names and credit

- Don't present a fork as Illogical Windows or ii-windows itself, or make it look official (name, setup, icon). Give it its own name and say it's based on Illogical Windows, with a link.
- Keep the credits and the license files: end-4's illogical-impulse, pctrade's end4-pC, Quickshell and everything in [THIRD-PARTY.md](THIRD-PARTY.md).

## Getting a fork listed

Open an issue with a link to the fork, what it adds, and how to install it on top of Illogical Windows.
